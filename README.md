# Mia Jewelry Chatbot Overview

## Core Purpose
The chatbot bridges the gap between online discovery and the tactile joy of in-store jewelry shopping. It automates routine tasks like finding a store or checking product details, freeing your team to focus on high-value "customer delight" interactions.

## Key Functional Pillars

### 1. Smart Store Locator
- **Instant Access:** Users share their location or pincode to get the exact address and operating hours of the nearest Mia boutique.
- **Direct Connectivity:** Provides the specific contact number for the store manager and a "WhatsApp Manager" button for direct, one-click communication.
- **Navigation:** Sends a direct Google Maps pin to guide the customer to your doorstep without friction.

### 2. Interactive Product Discovery
- **Assisted Browsing:** Instead of scrolling long web pages, customers use natural language or buttons to find items like "14kt gold studs for work".
- **Virtual Catalog:** Showcases rich media, including high-quality images and videos of collections like Mia Native or Nature’s Finest.
- **Inventory Check:** Real-time stock queries allow users to confirm if a specific ring size is available at their preferred branch before visiting.

### 3. Personalized Styling & Care
- **Virtual Stylist:** A guided "style interview" creates a customer profile to offer recommendations based on their unique taste, skin tone, or occasion.
- **The "Human" Touch:** A seamless "Handoff" feature lets customers transition from the bot to a live professional stylist or store manager for complex styling advice.
- **Jewelry Aftercare:** Proactively provides care guides and cleaning tips to ensure the jewelry stays sparkling long after the purchase.
